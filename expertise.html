About Us

Exceptional spaces.
Considered in every light.

With over 9 years of industry experience across high-end projects, OMEGALUX Studio brings together architectural lighting consultancy and carefully curated lighting supply. We shape refined residential, hospitality and commercial environments through technical precision, bespoke fixtures and a considered understanding of atmosphere.

Working closely with consultants, designers and contractors, we connect each creative vision with the right lighting solutions, from the first concept to final supply.

OUR VISION

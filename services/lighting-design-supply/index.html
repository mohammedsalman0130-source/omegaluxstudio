What we offer

One connected approach, from concept to specification.

Architectural lighting design and interiors

Lighting concepts, design layouts, comfortable ambient and task lighting, feature lighting and considered colour temperature and scene strategies coordinated with the architecture.

DIALux calculations and design review

Project-specific illuminance and uniformity calculations, lux plots, luminaire coordination and technical review against agreed project criteria and applicable guidance.

Façade lighting and landscape lighting

Wall washing, grazing, accent lighting, bollards, pathways and exterior lighting concepts with attention to glare, maintenance access, controls and environmental exposure.

Lighting products and supply

Selection and quotation of architectural luminaires, decorative lighting, chandeliers, pendants, wall lights and bespoke solutions based on client requirements and product availability.

Consultants, designers and contractors

Coordinated packages for real project decisions.

We review the brief and architectural drawings, define lighting objectives, prepare concept and detailed design information, assess photometric performance and support luminaire specification and selection. Scope and deliverables are agreed for each project.

Depending on the assignment, documentation may include lighting layouts, technical schedules, DIALux reports, luminaire specifications, lighting power density (LPD) calculations, lighting control intent and 3D visual concept presentations.

Lighting power density (LPD) calculations

Lighting power density expresses installed lighting power per unit area, typically watts per square metre. A reliable calculation depends on defined room boundaries, fixture counts, input wattage and the applicable compliance method.

Request a project quotation

Calculation inputs and outputs

Provide architectural drawings, room areas, lighting layouts and the proposed luminaire schedule. A scoped deliverable can include a room-by-room LPD worksheet with counts, wattages, areas and comparison against agreed limits.

Applicable criteria

The relevant Estidama or ASHRAE method and edition should be agreed for the project; space-by-space and building-area methods are not interchangeable. Clarify exclusions and control assumptions with the responsible consultant.

Frequently asked question

Is an LPD worksheet an authority approval?

No. It supports the project compliance review; final compliance depends on the applicable requirements and approving authority.

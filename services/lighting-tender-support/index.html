Lighting tender and BOQ support

Lighting tender packages often contain fixture types, performance criteria, drawings and quantities from different sources. We review the supplied information to identify the scope and clarify missing or inconsistent items before preparing a response.

Tender documentation

Support can include luminaire schedules, technical datasheet review, compliance comparison, proposed alternatives and supply quotations. DIALux calculations or layouts can be scoped separately where required.

Procurement decisions

Check the approved specification, optical distribution, mounting conditions, control protocol, dimensions and relevant manufacturer documentation before offering alternatives. Final acceptance remains with the appointed consultant and client.

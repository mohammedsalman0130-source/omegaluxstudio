Architectural façade lighting design

Exterior lighting should reveal the building rather than overwhelm it. We study elevations, architectural edges, materials, viewing distances and surrounding conditions to establish a hierarchy of light.

Concept and visual studies

Review optics, glare, spill light, mounting access, weather exposure and maintenance. Fixture protection ratings, controls and installation details are subject to the project specification and site conditions.

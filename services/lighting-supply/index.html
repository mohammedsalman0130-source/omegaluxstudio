Technical selection

Provide the luminaire schedule, drawings, preferred brands, quantities and performance requirements. We can review the relevant product data, mounting details, optical distribution, controls and proposed alternatives for consultant approval.

Supply coordination

For decorative fittings, confirm finish, dimensions and installation conditions. For architectural fittings, confirm wattage, lumen output, optics and driver compatibility. Availability, delivery and commercial terms are confirmed in the quotation.

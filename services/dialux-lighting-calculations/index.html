DIALux evo lighting calculations in the UAE

A DIALux model helps test whether a proposed luminaire arrangement meets the project lighting objectives. Geometry, calculation surfaces, reflectance assumptions and photometric files need to represent the proposed design rather than generic fixture values.

Request a project quotation

Calculation deliverables

Depending on scope, the report can include average and minimum illuminance, uniformity, calculation objects, false-colour plots and luminaire schedules. The criteria are agreed against applicable project standards and authority requirements.

Review and coordination

Check luminaire photometry, maintenance factor, mounting height, room use and working plane before interpreting results. Where required, update the layout after consultant comments; calculations are not a substitute for authority approval or site measurement.

Frequently asked question

What information is required?

DWG/DXF or dimensioned plans, ceiling heights, room uses, luminaire IES/LDT files and the required lighting criteria.

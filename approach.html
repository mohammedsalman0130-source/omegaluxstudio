01 / LIGHTING DESIGN

From an idea.
To a complete design.

SCROLL TO FOLLOW THE PROCESS ↓

01 / CONSULTATION

First, we listen.

We review the architecture, room uses, project stage, budget and programme with your team. We agree the scope, applicable criteria and the information needed to begin.

DELIVERABLES

Project brief · scope · design criteria

02 / CONCEPT DESIGN

A clear direction for light.

We develop the atmosphere, lighting hierarchy, colour temperature and scene intentions. Concept layouts connect the visual story to the architecture.

DELIVERABLES

Concept boards · lighting strategy · initial layouts

03 / CALCULATION & VALIDATION

Prove the performance.

We test the scheme using photometric data and agreed surface assumptions. Maintained illuminance, task-area uniformity and glare are reviewed against the project criteria.

DELIVERABLES

Calculation model · lux plots · technical review

04 / DETAILED COORDINATION

Resolve every connection.

We coordinate luminaire positions, mounting details, drivers and control zones with architectural and MEP information. Specifications and quantities become a coordinated package.

DELIVERABLES

Lighting plans · details · schedules · controls intent

05 / REVIEW & APPROVAL

Refine. Review. Resolve.

We present the design package for consultant and client review, track comments and incorporate agreed changes. Product alternatives are checked against the lighting intent.

DELIVERABLES

Review register · revised documents · approved selections

06 / DESIGN HANDOVER

Everything your team needs.

We issue the agreed final project design files in a structured handover. The package brings together layouts, calculations, luminaire specifications, schedules and control documentation.

DELIVERABLES

Final drawings · calculation reports · specifications · BOQ · agreed editable files

Delivery notes · receipt inspection · installation information

Support beyond delivery.

We coordinate product support and agreed commissioning assistance with the project team. Warranty, maintenance and spare-parts information complete the supply package.

DALI dimming and commissioning for Dubai hotels and Abu Dhabi villas

OMEGALUX Studio · 2 November 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

Follow a command to the light

When a guest presses a scene key, the input device reports the action, the application controller applies the programmed logic and the addressed driver changes the luminaire output.

A DALI bus supply powers communication on the line, while the LED driver supplies the light engine, so the MEP drawings should identify both functions even where one device combines them.

Check the exact model in the DALI Alliance product database and specify DT8 colour control where a tunable-white scene needs colour commands.

Test what happens near minimum

For a Dubai ballroom chandelier with replaceable lamps, test the complete lamp population on the proposed phase-cut interface because low-load behaviour can differ from a single-lamp bench test.

Commission the lowest stable scene by fading down, starting directly at that setting and recalling it after power restoration, noting flicker, dropout and differences between fittings.

Make savings measurable

In a glazed Abu Dhabi office, zone perimeter desks separately so the daylight sensor can reduce their output while deeper workstations retain the intended lux.

Record input power at full output and at the occupied scene to estimate operating energy from hours of use, while checking connected LPD separately under the applicable calculation method.

Hand over the address-to-room schedule, group assignments, scene values and controller backup so a replacement driver can be commissioned without rebuilding the floor’s programming.

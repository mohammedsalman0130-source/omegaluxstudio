    02 / UNDERSTAND THE NUMBERS
    01

    Lux is the amount.

    Illuminance measures light arriving on a surface. A maintained value allows for the reduction in performance over the maintenance period; it is not simply the initial output of new fittings.

    02

    Uniformity is the balance.

    U₀ compares the lowest point to the average on the same assessment grid. At an average of 500 lux, U₀ = 0.60 corresponds to a minimum of 300 lux. Use the task-specific value from the SLL schedule; immediate-surround and background guidance is separate.

    03

    The task sets the target.

    Storage, detailed inspection and reading need different conditions. The measurement plane matters too: a vertical rack face is different from a horizontal desk.

    About this SLL 2022 guide

    The 53 indoor workplace tables in §3.2 contain 328 searchable tasks and subtasks across industrial processes, offices, education, healthcare, hospitality, public buildings, retail and transport. Required and modified maintained illuminance remain separate; printed dashes remain unspecified.

    Task criteria are cited by table, reference and printed page. General reflectance guidance is from §3.1.2.2 (p. 32); surrounding/background illuminance and uniformity from §3.1.3.4–3.1.3.6 (pp. 34–36); colour-temperature groups from §3.1.7.2 (p. 45). CCT is presented as a task requirement only where the task note states one. The Sports and ADM guides retain their supplied editions.
